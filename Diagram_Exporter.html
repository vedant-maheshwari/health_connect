📊 Diagram Exporter

Download individual diagrams for your presentation

📝 How to Use:

  • Select a diagram from the dropdown below
  • Click "Download as PNG" to save as image (best for PPT)
  • Click "Download as SVG" to save as vector (scalable)
  • Click "Copy SVG Code" to get the raw SVG for editing
  • Right-click on diagram → "Save image as..." also works!
